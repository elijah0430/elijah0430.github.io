Ph.D. Student, Data Science

Jongwon Lim

I study natural language processing and large language models at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University. My work focuses on evaluation methods that make model behavior easier to inspect, especially factuality, linguistic capability, value expression, and tutoring dialogue.

About

Research interests

I build datasets, evaluation pipelines, and analyses for understanding LLM outputs. Recent topics include long-form hallucination evaluation, Korean and morphology-oriented linguistic benchmarks, value alignment, mechanistic analysis, and LLM-based tutoring systems.
